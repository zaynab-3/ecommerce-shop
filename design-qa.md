# Storefront and admin design QA

final result: passed

## Current implementation

The store keeps the approved four-column desktop/two-column phone catalog, horizontal home carousel, responsive gallery, and mobile side drawer. Its identity uses Space Grotesk, one owner-selected accent, black and white, light black-alpha product borders, and no decorative shadows. Genuine product colors and photos retain their colors. Header search/cart/favorite icons have transparent backgrounds and use the saved accent. Cart and favorite counts sit at the upper right of their 44px controls, update after server confirmation, and survive reload; favorites remain visible on phones. The header no longer includes the My cart label.

The desktop secondary navigation row is removed. **Delivery across Lebanon** now links to Help from the left of the announcement bar, replacing Delivery areas at checkout. The link remains visible beside Cash on delivery on phones. The phone drawer retains **Discover** and **Shop**. All categories, New arrivals, and Everyday value menu links remain removed. Active and hovered navigation/category links use bold text without underlines. Cash on delivery has no trailing arrow. Desktop breadcrumbs, product count, and stock/sort controls share one row above the sidebar and products. Shop pages on phones have the breadcrumb, pinned-All category track, then filters. Discover uses its home category grid without a second category strip.

Home category links have no filled icon circles or tile backgrounds. Only their icons use the saved accent; labels are regular black text and become bold on hover. Selected category navigation remains bold. Category chevrons and the arrow beside View all products in this section are removed. Uploaded SVG icons use the same surrounding color through an alpha mask while retaining the sandboxed external asset route.

Visible vertical desktop scrollbars use the saved accent with transparent tracks. Chromium/WebKit custom parts have an 8px rail and rounded inset thumbs that widen slightly on hover. The native thin-scrollbar color is available as the standards fallback. All horizontal scrollbar rails are hidden, including Top picks, categories, swatches, horizontal gallery thumbnails, and admin tables. Scrolling, carousel controls, keyboard access, and autoplay remain available. Phone/touch vertical scrollbars retain their hidden behavior.

Public store links, categories, pagination, and stock/sort filters fetch server-rendered content into the existing page. The address and title update through the History API; Back/Forward restore results and scroll without a document refresh. Existing content remains visible and fully opaque until the response is ready, with no spinner or blank loading layer. Older requests are cancelled; a failed request keeps the current page and shows an error notification. Cart/favorite mutations and navigation share a queue to preserve session state. Gallery, quantity, category, and carousel behavior work after replacement; old carousel timers/listeners/observers are cleaned up. Real links and GET forms still work without JavaScript. A short arrival slide keeps the header stationary; reduced-motion preferences suppress it and the product entry movements.

Top picks advances by one card every five seconds and loops at the end. Manual swiping and desktop arrows remain available, alongside an unframed accent pause/resume control. Automatic movement pauses on hover/focus/touch, with open dialogs, in hidden tabs, and outside the viewport. Reduced-motion preferences disable autoplay. The search dialog's submit and close icons use the accent without filled backgrounds, including hover.

## Cart and checkout

The rounded sheet sizes to its content up to 90% of the viewport, with a softly blurred backdrop and internal scrolling for longer orders. It first shows cart items, icon quantity/removal controls, subtotal, and **Proceed to checkout**. The redundant Your items heading and lonely continue-shopping arrow are removed; checkout actions have plain text labels. The second phase shows customer/delivery fields and an order review, with **Place order** as the final action. **Back to cart** preserves entered details and updates the review after quantity changes. Product colors and prices come from server-rendered snapshots; order prices and stock are validated on the server.

All checkout controls are 48px high, including the address and order-notes textareas. Rounded, lightly filled fields replace the underlines; focus changes the label and fill without drawing a rectangular border. Long text scrolls inside the same-height textarea. Field gaps, review thumbnails, totals, and legal text are compact; a subtle divider separates the review on phones. The phone field fixes +961 beside the formatted Lebanese number. Selecting a delivery area dismisses the phone picker and updates the fee/total while keeping checkout open. Back/quantity changes retain the draft. Email is absent; optional notes are labelled at the end of the label row. Checkout and confirmation contain no website footer. Confirmation uses the selected accent for its check and status badge.

## Search and catalog

The centered search dialog has one underline, debounced live suggestions, cancellation of older requests, and keyboard selection. Each result links to its product detail page. Typing, submitting, or closing search does not filter or navigate the underlying shop page. Candidate lookup is bounded and briefly cached; visibility and prices are checked fresh without opening a customer session. Product names render as text, with escaped literal SQL wildcard searches.

Product cards have slight borders, selectable colors above pricing, and a simple plus action beside the price. A circular accent ring identifies the selected swatch; the underline is removed. Current and crossed-out comparison prices sit beside one another in a single 44px purchase row, so discounts do not reserve a second line. Compact price sizing adapts to phone/carousel widths; plus controls retain their 44px targets. Color selection changes the linked image and cart color, including products with a single color. Product-detail Add to cart and Buy it now actions sit beside one another. Favorites stay unframed and fill with the selected accent when saved. Badges and five-second notifications also use the selected accent. Cart/favorite mutations do not refresh the page or reset scroll.

Image frames explicitly fill the card width, and image links fill those frames with centered contain sizing. The favorite and footer-plus glyphs are both 20px and share one right-hand anchor. Both controls remain 44px square and stay inside the card. The footer action has a reserved position independent of price width; the previous phone-specific negative margins are removed. Top picks, catalog, saved, and related cards use the same geometry.

Card image frames use a wider aspect ratio and a 156px height cap. Products without colors no longer reserve a color row. At 1440px the first catalog card is 340px tall, compared with 400px in the preceding revision; the related ring card is 220px wide and about 285px tall. Product gallery frames have definite 360/300/260px desktop/tablet/phone heights. Images use contain sizing, with a maximum rendered height of 312/252/220px respectively, so tall bracelet photos cannot enlarge the page.

All remains fixed beside a separate scrolling category track. The selected category is brought into view, bounded by the actual first/last links; the artificial edge spacers are removed, so Electronics follows All immediately. Icons retain their size, long names truncate, and the scrollbar stays hidden. Active storefront links use bold accent text on a transparent background. Close controls have a transparent background and accent icons, retaining 44px dialog/drawer targets.

## Footer, legal pages, and cookies

The benefits section stays white with black text and accent icons. The footer uses the saved accent background with automatic black/white foreground contrast. Privacy policy and Terms & conditions have their own responsive pages and links from help, the footer, and checkout review. No footer is inserted into the cart/checkout sheet.

The nonblocking cookie notice explains the existing essential shopping/security cookies. There are no advertising/analytics cookies or invented optional-tracking switches. Acknowledgement lasts 180 days, survives navigation/reload, and can be reopened from the footer. The encrypted cookie is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and Secure on HTTPS. The endpoint retains CSRF/session protection and serializes with cart mutations. The HTML form works without JavaScript; the footer then links to the cookie explanation.
